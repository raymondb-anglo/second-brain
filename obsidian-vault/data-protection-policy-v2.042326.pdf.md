# Data Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes a comprehensive framework for how Anglo Singapore International School (Anglo) collects, uses, stores, secures, and disposes of personal and institutional data. It ensures compliance with the Thailand Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and international best practices. The policy also outlines the rules for data retention, disposal, and incident management.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees, faculty, administrative staff, contractors, and interns.
- Students, parents, guardians, and alumni.
- All third-party vendors, service providers, and consultants with access to school data.
- Data in both physical and electronic forms across all campuses (Sukhumvit 64, Sukhumvit 31, Nakhon Ratchasima).

## 3. Definitions
- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
- **Sensitive Personal Data**: Data such as health, religion, race, biometrics, and other categories defined under PDPA.
- **Processing**: Any operation performed on data such as collection, storage, retrieval, transfer, sharing, or destruction.
- **Data Subject**: An individual whose data is processed by Anglo - students, parents, and staff.
- **Vendor**: Any third party providing goods or services that handles student and staff personal data outside school premises.
- **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**: A contractual agreement outlining data protection obligations.
- **Retention Period**: The legally or operationally defined timeframe for keeping data.
- **Data Breach**: Unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data.
- **Incident**: Any event that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of data or systems.
- **Automated Decision-Making (ADM)**: Any process where significant outcomes—such as academic grading or disciplinary action—are made by AI with limited human oversight.
- **Generative AI**: AI that produces new text, images, code, or other media based on training data.

## 4. Guiding Principles
Anglo Singapore International School is committed to ensuring that all data processing follows these principles:
1. **Lawfulness & Fairness**: Collected with a legitimate basis (legal, contractual, or consent).
2. **Transparency**: Stakeholders informed of purpose, use, and retention.
3. **Purpose Limitation**: Data used only for its original purpose.
4. **Minimization**: Collect only necessary data.
5. **Accuracy**: Maintain up-to-date records.
6. **Security**: Safeguards to prevent unauthorized access.
7. **Retention Control**: Store only for the required duration.
8. **Accountability**: Senior Leadership Team (SLT), Data Protection Officer (DPO), and all staff are responsible for compliance.
9. **Human-Centric**: Appropriate degree of human involvement in automated decisions.

## 5. Policy
- Anglo processes personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently, with clear purpose limitations.
- A "human-in-the-loop" approach is used for all high-stakes decisions to ensure fairness and accountability.
- Personal data will only be collected where necessary for academic, administrative, legal, or safeguarding functions.
- Data subject rights under PDPA will be upheld, including:
  - **Right of Access and Portability**: To request a copy of personal data or its transfer to another controller.
  - **Right to Correction**: To rectify inaccurate or incomplete records.
  - **Right to Erasure**: To request deletion when no legal basis for retention exists.
  - **Right to Object**: To refuse data usage for marketing or specific optional activities.
  - **Right to Withdrawal of Consent**: To retract consent at any time.
  - **Right to Object to Automated Decision-Making (ADM)**: Not to be subjected to decisions based purely on automated processing where it produces a legal or similarly significant effect.
- Consent shall be obtained where required and may be withdrawn at any time.
- Sensitive personal data shall only be processed with explicit consent or where legally permitted.
- International transfers of data will be permitted only where adequate protections are in place.
- Access to data concerning data subjects who have already left the school must undergo proper authorization before processing.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Senior Leadership Team (SLT)**: Approves this policy and ensures resources for compliance.
- **Head of School**: Oversees implementation and accountability.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: 
  - Maintains and updates the policy.
  - Reviews and approves DPAs.
  - Conducts training and awareness activities.
  - Monitors compliance through submission of evidence.
  - Manages breach investigations and data subject requests.
- **Heads of Department (HoDs)**: Ensure department-level compliance.
- **IT Department**: Verify technical security of vendor systems.
- **All Staff**: Follow protocols and report breaches.
- **Vendors/Third Parties**: Sign Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) and comply with standards.

## 7. Data Collection Procedures
- **Admissions**: Student/parent data collected only via official forms with consent.
- **Academic Records**: Attendance, assessments, and portfolios managed through the Student Information System (SIS).
- **Health Records**: Collected by the Infirmary Department; stored separately with restricted access.
- **Financial Records**: Handled by the Accounting Department, limited to tuition and billing.
- **Media Consent**: Parents provide consent for student images/videos.
- **CCTV Monitoring**: Retained for 30 days for security purposes.

## 8. Data Storage and Security
- **Physical Records**: 
  - Locked storage with restricted key access.
  - Sign-in/out logs for file access.
- **Digital Records**:
  - Two-factor authentication for applications and systems accessible outside the school network.
  - Servers are only accessible internally.
  - Encrypted backups are performed regularly.
  - Role-based access control - Access to school systems and data is granted strictly on a need-to-know basis.

## 9. Vendor Management
- Vendors handling school data must demonstrate compliance with PDPA and international data protection standards.
- Due diligence covering security certifications and data protection practices must be conducted before engagement.
- A DPA must be signed before data is shared.
- Vendors must submit auditing evidence and data training policy to Anglo.
- High-risk vendors must be reviewed annually, and relationships must be terminated if they fail to meet compliance obligations.

## 10. Data Sharing and Transfer
- **Internal**: Shared only with staff requiring access upon approved request.
- **External**: Provided to regulators/accreditation bodies or higher governing authority as required.
- **Third-Party Vendors**: Only with a signed DPA.

## 11. Data Retention and Disposal
| Data Type               | Retention Period                           | Disposal Method                    |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Student Records         | Permanent (archival) 10 years post-graduation | Archived securely / Shredding     |
| Staff Records           | 7 years post-employment                   | Shredding / secure wipe           |
| Finance Records         | 7 years                                   | Secure disposal                   |
| CCTV Footage            | 30 days (unless needed)                  | Automatic overwrite                |
| Health Records          | Duration of enrollment + 5 years          | Secure destruction                 |

## 12. Rights of Data Subjects
- **Access**: Request a copy of data.
- **Correction**: Request updates to inaccurate data.
- **Deletion**: Request erasure if no legal basis for retention.
- **Objection**: Refuse use for marketing or optional activities.
- **Process**: Submit request → DPO acknowledges in 2 working days → response in 3 to 5 working days; refusal requires written justification.

## 13. Data Breach & Violation Protocol
- **Detection**: Report immediately to DPO; complete Incident Report Form.
- **Containment**: DPO isolates affected systems/accounts.
- **Assessment**: DPO + IT/SLT assess scope, type, and risk level.
- **Notification**: 
  - SLT within 24 hours.
  - Affected individuals + regulators (if required) within 72 hours.
- **Remediation**: Restore systems, reset access, close security gaps, and retrain staff.
- **Documentation**: All breaches logged in the Data Breach Register.
- **Disciplinary Action**: 
  - Minor negligence → warning + training.
  - Serious negligence → suspension, investigation.
  - Intentional misuse → termination/legal action.
- **Post-Incident Review**: Meeting within 2 weeks; lessons applied to policy.
- **Transparency**: Communicated to affected parties and documented for accreditation evidence.

## 14. Staff Training and Awareness
- **Induction Training**: For all new staff within the first week.
- **Annual Refreshers**: For all staff once per academic year with updates + case studies.
- **Departmental Briefings**: High-risk roles trained at least once per semester.
- **Breach Drills**: Annual simulation exercises; reports filed with SLT.
- **Evidence**: Signed acknowledgments, attendance sheets, training materials.

## 15. Monitoring and Review
- **Bi-Annual Audits**: Conducted by DPO (Dec/June) with IT, HR, Finance; findings recorded in Audit Report.
- **Annual Policy Review**: By DPO + SLT at the end of the academic year; revised version circulated to staff.
- **Reporting to SLT**: Annual summary including audits, breaches, training compliance, and resource needs.

## 16. Documentation
Anglo maintains full evidence to demonstrate compliance:
- Consent Forms
- Training Logs
- Data Access Requests
- Data Disposal Logs
- Breach Register
- Vendor DPAs
- Audit Reports

All evidence is securely stored by the DPO, with copies filed in HR, Finance, or Registrar’s office as appropriate.
